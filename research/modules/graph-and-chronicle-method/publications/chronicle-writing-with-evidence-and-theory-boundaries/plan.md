# Paper Plan: Chronicle Writing with Evidence and Theory Boundaries

Paper ID: `LEXIS-PAPER-007`

Status: planned, not written.

## Research Question

How should LEXIS chronicles explain evidence, inference, reconstruction,
alternatives, uncertainty, graph paths, and source limits without sounding more
settled than the reviewed slice permits?

## Scope

Included:

- Chronicle sections in `chronicle-output.md`.
- Source-limit and overclaim wording.
- Graph summary as input, not proof.
- Negative wording cases for reconstruction, script lanes, and source-limited
  claims.

Excluded:

- Writing a real chronicle.
- Publishing public-facing source claims.
- Creating report templates or CLI output.
- Replacing role review with prose style guidance.

## Expected Outputs

- Chronicle section adequacy review.
- Wording rule refinements.
- Required negative wording cases.
- Source-limit phrasing requirements.
- DCR recommendations if `COMMUNICATIONS_STRATEGY.md` or review checklists need
  updates.

## Expected Negative Findings

- "Proves" language is invalid for reconstruction or inference.
- Graph paths do not by themselves prove historical relationship.
- Source-limited claims require visible limitations.
- Reader-friendly prose cannot collapse alternatives.
