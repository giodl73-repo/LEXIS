Status: planned.

## Required Review Lanes

| Role | Review responsibility |
|---|---|
| L-1 Language Historian | Check that graph and chronicle output preserve bounded historical scope. |
| L-2 Etymology Reviewer | Check that edge and claim distinctions survive graph and prose. |
| L-3 Phonology Reviewer | Check reconstructed sound and unavailable data wording. |
| L-4 Script Systems Reviewer | Check script-lane graph/prose separation from sound and meaning shifts. |
| L-5 Source Custody Reviewer | Check source posture preservation through graph and chronicle output. |
| L-6 Graph Systems Reviewer | Check local graph baseline and RLINE adoption criteria. |
| L-7 Product Chronicle Reviewer | Check readability, evidence/theory separation, and narrative flow. |
| L-8 Software Assurance Reviewer | Check diagnostics, fixtures, validation commands, and promotion blockers. |

## Review Rule

Plans can be accepted as planning input. Paper findings cannot authorize graph
output, RLINE adoption, chronicle text, or public claims until the paper is
written and reviewed.
