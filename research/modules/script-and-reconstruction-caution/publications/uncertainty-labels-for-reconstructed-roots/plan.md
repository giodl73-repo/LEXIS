# Paper Plan: Uncertainty Labels for Reconstructed Roots

Paper ID: `LEXIS-PAPER-005`

Status: planned, not written.

## Research Question

Are LEXIS's uncertainty labels sufficient for reconstructed roots, reconstructed
sounds, competing theories, and source-limited reconstruction claims?

## Scope

Included:

- PIE root mini-slice implications.
- Semitic root-pattern reconstruction only if later scope requires it.
- `Root`, `SoundFeature`, and `TheoryClaim` records.
- Chronicle overclaim controls around reconstructed forms.

Excluded:

- Selecting a real PIE root.
- Choosing descendant wordforms.
- Creating reconstruction fixtures.
- Writing public reconstruction claims.
