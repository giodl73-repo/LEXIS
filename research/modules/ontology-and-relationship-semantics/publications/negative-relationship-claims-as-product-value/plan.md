# Paper Plan: Negative Relationship Claims as Product Value

Paper ID: `LEXIS-PAPER-010`

Status: planned, not written.

## Research Question

How can LEXIS make rejected, unknown, source-limited, and folk-etymology claims
useful to readers without overstating what is known?

## Scope

Included:

- Negative relationship claims for `LEXIS-SLICE-005`.
- First-slice negative claim requirements.
- Chronicle wording for "not related", "unknown", "unsupported", and
  "source-limited".
- Product value of preserving rejected alternatives.

Excluded:

- Proving absence of every possible relation.
- Publishing folk-etymology examples as claims.
- Writing public chronicles.
- Creating graph output.

## Expected Outputs

- Negative claim taxonomy for product output.
- Wording rules for rejected, unknown, unsupported, and source-limited cases.
- Fixture requirements for negative relationship claims.
- Chronicle guidance for explaining non-relationships.

## Expected Negative Findings

- "Not related" may need narrower wording than "no relationship exists".
- Folk-etymology examples require careful source and public-claim posture.
- Negative claims need evidence or rationale, not only assertion.
