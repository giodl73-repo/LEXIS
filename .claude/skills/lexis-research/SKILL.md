# LEXIS Research

Use this skill before broad LEXIS adoption of a source family, linguistic theory,
dataset, graph metric, source-custody route, or public claim.

## Research requirements

- Separate attested evidence from reconstruction and theory.
- Name source rights posture before ingestion or redistribution.
- Distinguish descent, cognacy, borrowing, calque, coincidence, and uncertain
  relationships.
- Record uncertainty labels and competing theories.
- Check whether the decision belongs in LEXIS, RLINE, CANON, FLETCH, PROOF,
  CROP, PEBBLE, or a downstream consumer.
- Produce a short decision note or VTRACE-stage finding before implementation.
