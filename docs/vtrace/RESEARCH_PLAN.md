# LEXIS VTRACE Research Plan

Status: settled.

The controlling research backlog lives at [`../../RESEARCH_PLAN.md`](../../RESEARCH_PLAN.md).
This VTRACE artifact records the stage placement and promotion rules.

## Stage Placement

LEXIS research belongs in S1 with `PROBLEM_SPACE_MAP.md`, `DOMAIN_BACKLOG.md`,
and `SPEC_MODEL.md`. It turns source-heavy language-history questions into
reviewed requirements input before fixtures, graph output, chronicles, or
publisher artifacts exist.

## Research-To-Requirement Rule

Research findings remain advisory until they are accepted through a DCR and
reflected in controlled artifacts such as `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `SPEC_MODEL.md`,
`SOURCE_BASIS.md`, `LANGUAGE_SLICE_PACKAGES.md`, `WORK_PACKAGES.md`,
`SCENARIO_MODEL.md`, or future implementation specs.
