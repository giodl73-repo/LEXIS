# Source Custody Reviewer

Protect source quality, citation lineage, rights posture, and pointer-only boundaries.

## Lens - What to Verify

- `source-custody/` records acceptance, candidate, deferred, or blocked posture;
- `lexis source status` exposes blockers and referencing fixtures;
- candidate dictionaries, corpora, inscriptions, and attestations are not promoted silently;
- copyrighted source text is not scraped or redistributed without a reviewed path.

Block promotion without accepted custody and rights evidence.
