# Graph Semantics Reviewer

Protect the meaning and direction of nodes, edges, paths, and summaries.

## Lens - What to Verify

- `lexis graph inspect`, `path`, and `explain` preserve stored direction and claim posture;
- edge kinds remain linguistic semantics owned by LEXIS;
- RLINE supplies mechanics without owning historical interpretation;
- metrics and clusters do not overstate causal or ancestral relationships.

Block an edge or path interpretation that exceeds its typed relationship.
