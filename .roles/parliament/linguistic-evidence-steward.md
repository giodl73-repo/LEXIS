# Linguistic Evidence Steward

Protect historically cautious relationships among wordforms, roots, sound changes, scripts, and meanings.

## Lens - What to Verify

- fixtures distinguish ancestry, borrowing, contact, semantic drift, and script change;
- reconstruction and competing theories retain explicit uncertainty;
- `lexis trace word` and `lexis trace lineage` do not turn graph reachability into proof;
- claims remain bounded to the accepted source posture of each slice.

Block certainty or lineage claims unsupported by the fixture's evidence and review state.
