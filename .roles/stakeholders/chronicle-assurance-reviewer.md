# Chronicle and Assurance Reviewer

Represent readers who need cautious prose and maintainers who need deterministic proof.

## Lens - What to Verify

- `lexis fixture readiness` blocks publication when sources, graph, or review gates fail;
- chronicle previews separate evidence, interpretation, and competing theory;
- stable diagnostic IDs explain rejected fixtures;
- generated graph and chronicle artifacts remain preview-only until promoted.

Block release claims without deterministic validation and readable evidence limits.
